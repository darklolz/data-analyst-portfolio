08 Key Findings & Insights
📚

Study Hours is the #1 Predictor

With a Pearson correlation of r = 0.804, study hours/week is by far the strongest predictor of final score. No other variable comes close.

🏆

26–30 hrs/week → 100% Pass Rate

Students studying 26–30 hours per week achieved a perfect pass rate. Students studying 1–5 hrs/week passed only 18.8% of the time.

📉

Most Students Scored Lower on Final

61.6% of students (308 out of 500) scored lower on their final than their previous assessment. Average score dropped by 7 points.

🎓

High School Parent Education Wins

Surprisingly, students whose parents hold a High School education have the highest pass rate (70.5%), beating Bachelor's (53.6%), Master's, and PhD holders.

🌐

Internet Access Has Minimal Impact

Pass rate with internet: 65.7% vs without: 63.4%. Only a 2.3-point difference — internet access alone does not explain academic performance.

⚽

Extracurricular Activities Slightly Help

Students with extracurricular: 66.0% pass rate vs 63.2% without. A small but positive correlation — activities may build discipline or morale.

📊

Attendance Matters — But Less Than Study

Attendance correlation with final score: r = 0.241. Students with 90–100% attendance had a 74.5% pass rate vs 54.9% for 50–60% attendance.

⚖️

Gender Gap is Minimal

Male students averaged 56.5 final score and 65.6% pass rate; females averaged 55.5 and 63.7%. Differences are statistically small.
